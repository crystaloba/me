About

Research, systems, accessibility

I started in public health, then moved into clinical research, which is what led me to UX research and design. That path taught me to look at both the current state and the historical state of a system before trying to improve or refine it.

My work spans agency projects for manufacturers and local businesses, civic tech research at Coforma, and now enterprise partnership experiences at WGU. The common thread is untangling complex journeys and designing to WCAG 2.2 AA from the start.
