Medic: medication manager (redesign prototype)
medıc

This is a redesign of the Medic medication app. It uses a weekly pill organizer as its structure. Everything here is sample data and runs in your browser.

Jump to a screen

Tap the check on a dose, add a medicine, log how you feel, then look a medicine up in the glossary. The sample content is for a design prototype and is not medical advice. Ask your pharmacist or doctor about your own medicines.
